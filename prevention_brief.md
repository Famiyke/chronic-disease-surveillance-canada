# Chronic Disease Surveillance | Illustrative Prevention Brief

> **Portfolio simulation.** The prevalence rates and geographic patterns generated for this project are not verified PHAC CCDSS estimates. Do not use them for real allocation or epidemiologic reporting.

## Decision question
How might a public health team compare condition trends by year, geography and age group?

## Work demonstrated
- Prepared simulated chronic-disease indicator tables.
- Applied Python and SQL for trends, stratified comparisons and reporting.
- Designed a demonstration burden index and reporting outputs.

## Interpretation and limitations
The original materials contain inconsistent numerical comparisons. For example, a stated 12.3% rate is lower than a 13.7% comparison rate. Cross-population comparisons require consistent years, case definitions, age ranges, denominators and age-standardization. Trends generated by the model should not be attributed to PHAC.

## Next steps for real surveillance
1. Download published tables from the [CCDSS data tool](https://health-infobase.canada.ca/ccdss/data-tool/).
2. Document extraction dates, measures and adjustments.
3. Recalculate national and provincial values with matched denominators.
4. Review uncertainty and population coverage before proposing funding priorities.
