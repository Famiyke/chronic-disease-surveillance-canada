# Source validation boundary

The simulated data uses demonstration age groups such as 20–44, 45–64, 65–74 and 75+. These are **not** the default CCDSS public-data age aggregations and cannot be assumed comparable with them.

Authoritative CCDSS methodology: https://health-infobase.canada.ca/ccdss/Methods

PHAC explains that its standard life-course age groups include 1–19, 20–34, 35–49, 50–64, 65–79 and 80+, with condition-specific exceptions. Published estimates also apply disclosure suppression, including unrounded counts below 10.

Use the CCDSS source tables for actual estimates and keep their disease definitions, age groups, observation years and age-adjustment method. The generated prevalence numbers in this repository are not verified official CCDSS outputs.

**Validation status:** Actual PHAC prevalence rates not independently reproduced.
